SceneCard + Slot · a scene is a card you insert

From the Yoto reference: content is chosen by putting a card in. A scene (Movie night, Away, Good night) is printed on a small card of light stock, one step lighter than the plastic. A room or a device has a slot; the inserted card stands half out with its name readable and the slot's lamp lights. Default to avoid: a grid of rounded gradient tiles with big emoji.
