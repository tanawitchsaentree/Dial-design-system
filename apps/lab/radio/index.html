Radio — three ways to show the one chosen out of a few

Same ink, same light. The chosen option must differ from the others by shape or position, not only by tone. Never orange. Every option is a 44px target.

A · round key with an ink dot

Heating mode

Sibling of Checkbox: a round key at height, a dot of printing ink on its face when chosen.

B · legend with an index bar

Heating mode

P7 taken literally: all the words stand printed, the chosen one in full ink and medium with a bar. No part to press, so nothing says the words are targets.

C · ball seated in a well

Heating mode

A recess holds a raised ball when chosen. It reads as a detent, but the empty well looks like an input field and the part cannot be pressed like a key.
