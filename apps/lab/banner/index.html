Banner · three ways to keep a condition on screen

"The hub is offline. Controls will work again when it reconnects." Status is the Toast glyph and a word; no stripe on the start edge, no tinted disc, no orange.

A · strip of the case (picked)

A plastic band at unit height, in the flow at the top of the region it speaks for. Keys at the end; wraps under the message when narrow.

B · printed line

No body, printed straight on the page with a seam. Quiet, but a condition that blocks controls disappears into the page text.

C · engraved plate

The words in a recessed plate. A second recess beside the display window (P2), and it reads as a field to type into.
