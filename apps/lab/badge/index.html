Badge — three ways to print a status on the case

Every tone is a glyph with its own shape and a word: positive a dot, warning a triangle, critical a square, info a diamond, neutral a ring. None is orange. Status is never colour alone.

A · printed lamp and word

Flat printing on the plastic: no box, no fill, no shadow. The glyph is a printed lamp; the word sits on the label line.

B · word in a printed frame

A hairline frame printed around glyph and word. It turns a status into something that looks like a key, so people try to press it.

C · annunciator window

A small LCD window per status. Every window is a second display in the unit, and P2 allows one; the tones also lose their mark colours.
