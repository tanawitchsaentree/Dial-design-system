EmptyState · what a room or list shows when it has nothing yet

"No devices in the kitchen yet." A printed line, one sentence of what to do, one key; the quiet drawing is made of the system's own parts (an unlit LCD, an empty grille field), never an illustration.

A · printed on the case (picked)

Start-aligned on the room's inset line: the drawing, the line, the sentence, the key. The key is orange only because adding a device is this room's main action.
