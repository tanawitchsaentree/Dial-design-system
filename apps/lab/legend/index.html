Legend: three ways to print every choice

P7 The legend is the state. Every choice is printed; the one in use is lit (full ink, medium weight, one ink index mark), the rest wait one step lighter. Turn the knob (drag, or Tab and arrows) and the lit word walks; click a word and the knob turns to it. One tab stop per value: the knob or fader is it, its aria-valuetext is the word.

A · column beside the knob (washer)

Programme

The synth panel's printed column. The words keep one place; only the mark slides on the spring.

B · arc around the knob (fan)

Fan

Each word sits at its detent's angle, so the knob's index points at it. Five words over 180°.

C · row under a fader (speaker)

Source

A three-way slide switch. Each word sits under its stop; the fader is the one tab stop.
