Toast — three ways to say what just happened

Each toast is a raised body under the single top-left light. Status is a printed glyph and a word, never a coloured dot or an icon disc, and never orange. The close key is a 44px round key. What changes is the silhouette and where the status is printed.

A · printed strip, one line

DoneLamp turned off
FailedWasher didn't answer
CheckFilter needs cleaning soon

Short: one line on a laptop, wraps to two on a phone. The word sits right beside its glyph like a label printed next to a lamp.

B · OLED window inside the case

DoneLamp turned off
FailedWasher didn't answer

Reads as a device. But the dark window is the dark snackbar again, and the glyph loses its tone (white on black) so the word does all the work.

C · status line above, keys below

Done
Lamp turned off
Failed
Washer didn't answer. Try again in a minute.

Room for a longer message, but three rows for "Lamp turned off" is a card, not a toast; it covers more of the screen for the same five seconds.
