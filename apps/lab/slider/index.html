Slider — three faders from the same material

Same matte ABS, same light, same spring (mass 1.4, stiffness 520, damping 46). Drag a cap or Tab to it and use arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home and End. The cap follows the hand and is released into the nearest detent; under Reduce motion it jumps at once. No part is orange: the rotary is the unit's loud part. Values estimated from chat.

A · ruler fader ("audio 1")

Volume

A narrow groove with a ruler above it and numerals below. Reads like a tape deck's level fader.

B · channel fader

Volume

The whole 44px track is one recess and the cap sits inside it. Heavier; the well competes with the display.

C · notch rail

Volume

Each detent is a recessed notch and a round cap drops between them, even while dragging. Only works with few steps.
