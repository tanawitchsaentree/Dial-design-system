Wiring · the relation between the thermostat, the heater and the night schedule

One room screen. The thermostat knob sets the heater; the schedule after 23:00 lowers it to 18. Night mode is active in every variant; the second relation waits. Four mechanisms of showing the relation, never decoration.

A · schematic face (printed box + one-bend ink lines on the module grid)

The heating group sits in one printed box. Lines run on 8px rows with right angles and at most one bend, end in a small node on each part, carry one lowercase label, and never cross a word. Active line full ink, waiting line secondary ink.

B · bus rail (every relation taps one printed rail along the foot of the face)

Each part drops a line straight down to a shared rail; labels sit on the rail between taps. Fewer bends, but the rail reads as one circuit, so which part drives which is lost.

C · numbered key (no lines; matching printed numerals and a relation key)

Each relation gets a numeral printed beside both of its ends, and the sentences are printed under the parts like a manual's legend. Quiet, but the eye has to travel twice to join the two ends.
