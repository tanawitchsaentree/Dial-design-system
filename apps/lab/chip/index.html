Chip — three ways a small key shows it is chosen

Filters and quick presets ("Eco", "Night", "Away") on one unit. Same matte ABS, same light, same spring, travel 3px. None is orange: orange belongs to the unit's one main control. Chosen must differ in shape or depth, not only in colour.

A · latching key with a lamp

KitchenHall

Like radio keys that lock down: a chosen chip stays pressed, its lamp lights and its word turns medium. Removable chips carry their own small round key.

B · raised tag with a printed ring

The chip stays at its height; a 2px ink ring marks the chosen one. The ring is the same weight as the focus ring, so focus and chosen are hard to tell apart.

C · keys on a shared rail

Only the chosen key stands up out of a recessed rail. It reads as pick-one, which is SegmentedControl's job, and chips may be many-of-many.
