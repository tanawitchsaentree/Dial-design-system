Meter · a static reading of a level in a known range

A meter reads a level that the person does not set: battery, air quality, water hardness, signal. It is not a task's progress (Progress) and not an activity field (Grille). Regions are told by a glyph and a word, never by colour alone, and nothing is orange.

A · printed scale and needle

A narrow window with printed ticks, like a radio's tuning scale. One ink needle marks the reading; dashed marks split the regions and their words print under them.

B · LCD bar segments

Segments in a grey-green window, like a charger's level. Clear, but a second display window competes with the unit's one (P2).

C · grille holes

A row of recessed holes lit in ink. Reads as Grille with a scale, and the coarse step cannot show a precise reading.
