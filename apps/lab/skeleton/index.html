Skeleton — four ways to hold the size of a device card, a list row and a reading while it loads

The house is fetching its devices. The region says aria-busy; the placeholder itself is hidden from assistive tech. P6 lets nothing move while idle, so a looping shimmer is out; C shows the slowest motion we could defend, to see whether it earns its place.

A · the unlit device

Every part draws as it looks with the power off: ghost segments 88:88 in the window, unlit holes, flat plastic where the knob and key will stand, flat blocks the height of a printed line. Still in every motion setting.

B · flat blocks only

Every part becomes the same grey rectangle. Still, but the window, the knob and the holes lose their shape, so it is the generic placeholder again with the shimmer taken out.

C · slow warm-up

A's parts breathe from 45% to full over 3.2s, like a display warming up. Slow, yet a page of twenty rows breathing in step is idle motion, which P6 rules out.

D · registration outlines

Dashed hairlines where parts will go, like a printer's layout. Reads as a drop zone or an empty slot to fill, not as content on its way.
