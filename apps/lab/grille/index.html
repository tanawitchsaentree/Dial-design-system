Grille — three hole fields that are the meter

Every hole is a recess in the body. A lit hole is ink at full step, never orange. The holes are the reading, so every grille has a name and a value for assistive tech. Press "Step" to change the level; holes fill in reading order in 120ms. Values estimated from chat.

A · rectangle field, columns fill left to right

Volume

A level meter: 16 × 4 holes, whole columns light from the start of the line. Same grid as the anchor card's grille.

B · circle field, rows fill bottom to top

Fan speed

A speaker-style round field: the level rises like liquid in the circle. Holes outside the circle are not drawn.

C · sparse strip, one hole per step

Wi-Fi signal

Five big holes for a coarse reading (signal, filter life). Fewer holes, each one a step you can count.
