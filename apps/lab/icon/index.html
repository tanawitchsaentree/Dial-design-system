Icon — four ways to draw dial's glyphs

A and D draw each glyph as round dots on a 7 × 7 matrix whose dot is two thirds of its pitch, the Grille's own ratio (8px hole on a 12px pitch). B uses a 9 × 9 matrix with the same ratio. C draws a hairline stroke on the 2-unit step of a 24-unit grid. D keeps A's dots and lets them grow at 16px only, like optical sizing on type. Every glyph uses currentColor. Each system is shown at 16, 20 and 24px, on the plastic, on a key and beside a word, in light and dark.

Legibility test (measured with a 1x and 2x screenshot of every glyph on surface.solid, light and dark): for each dot, the strongest pixel in its cell against the plastic. D passes at 16px, so no stroke variant is offered.
