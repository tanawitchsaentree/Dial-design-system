TimePicker — four ways to set when the night schedule starts

Home-iot scenario: "Night mode starts at 22:30". Each field is a spinbutton: arrows step it, PageUp and PageDown step by ten minutes or three hours, Home and End reach the ends, digits type in. No orange: the time is a setting, not the unit's one loud control. A clock-face dialog and the browser's time input are the defaults to avoid.
