Each variant on a 375px phone (rises from the bottom over a scrim) and on a wide screen (a panel by the card's key, like Menu). Light theme; switch with ?dark.

A · lid with a key (picked)

One docked body, family of Sheet. Rows are Menu's printing with glyphs; Remove comes last after a gap with the critical square lamp and its word. Cancel is a full-width plastic key under a gap. On a wide screen the same rows open as a panel by the key, flipped or shifted to stay whole, with Escape and a click away as the way out.

B · two bodies

The actions on one floating body and Cancel on a second one under it: the iOS layout in plastic. Two bodies for one decision, and the inset margins waste a phone's width.

C · key bank

Every action is a raised capsule key, stacked. Four keys of equal weight make Remove as easy to hit as Rename, and a lid of keys reads as a form to fill.
