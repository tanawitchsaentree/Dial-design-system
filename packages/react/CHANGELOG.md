# dial-react changelog

One line per change, `- Component: what changed`, under a heading of the version and date, newest first. The docs read this file for every component's history.

## 0.0.0 (2026-11-01)

- Button: First release. A capsule key with kind key or primary and sizes md and lg.
- Switch: First release. A slide switch with a cap in a recessed groove, printed position words and a lamp; role switch, Space and Enter throw it.
- Text: First release. Printing on the case, five sizes, two inks, regular and medium, tabular figures.
- Heading: First release. Levels h1 to h4 at the display, headline, title and emphasis sizes, medium weight, primary ink.
- IconButton: First release. A round 44px key with a printed icon, a required label as its accessible name, kind key or primary, size md or lg.
- Checkbox: First release. A small square key over a native checkbox, an ink tick when on and a dash when mixed, the label printed beside it.
- Display: First release. The recessed state window, LCD 7-segment or OLED text, a polite live region.
- Grille: First release. A perforated field whose recessed holes are a level meter, role meter with a required label.
- Slider: First release. A fader with a plastic cap in a recessed groove, a printed ruler, detents and the material's spring.
- Rotary: First release. The knob, a disc with one index line over a tick ring of detents across 270 degrees, primary (orange) or key.
- Input: First release. A recessed well the full width of its column, label printed above, hint, error with a glyph, unit suffix, md and lg.
- Select: First release. A select-only combobox (WAI-ARIA APG) in Input's well, opening a plastic listbox panel, type-ahead, controlled or uncontrolled, md and lg.
- SegmentedControl: First release. A bank of latching keys, like a radio's band keys; the chosen key is shown by depth, a lit ink lamp and a medium word.
- Tabs: First release. Section names printed in a row with one ink index mark under the chosen one; WAI-ARIA tabs with automatic or manual activation.
- Dialog: First release. A sheet on the native modal dialog that asks one question, with focus to the first field or plastic key and back to the opener.
- Toast: First release. A plastic strip with a glyph and a status word in four tones, plus ToastProvider and useToast with a 5 s minimum timer.
- Chip: First release of Chip, a small capsule key for filters and presets that latches down with a lit lamp, or carries a round remove key.
- Badge: First release of Badge, a status printed on the case as a lamp shaped by its tone and a word, with a count form.
- Progress: First release of Progress, a recessed groove that fills with ink, with a still indeterminate form.
- Card: First release. The plastic body of a unit, corner 0.16 of the short side capped at corner.panel, padding sm/md/lg/unit, header, footer, wordmark and model.
- DeviceCard: First release. One appliance from anchor A, with Display, the orange Rotary or a primary key, keys, Grille meter, Slider fader and printing; layouts a, tall, compact.
- RangeSlider: First release. Two plastic caps in one ruler groove that stop at each other, each its own slider with keys and the spring.
- Scrubber: First release. The position of a track as a cap in the fader groove, with chapter marks, the buffered stretch and the times printed under it.
- Meter: First release. A printed scale with one ink needle, regions told by a glyph and a word.
- Sheet: First release. A modal panel with a device's full controls, docked to the bottom edge on a tall screen with a printed ink bar to drag it down, and a unit at the lower end on a wide screen; family of Dialog.
- Banner: First release. A plastic strip in the flow that says a condition with the Toast glyph and a word, one plastic action key and an optional dismiss key; no stripe, no tint, no orange.
- EmptyState: First release. A printed line, one sentence and one key, with an optional drawing of an unlit LCD or an empty grille.
- List: First release. Rows of devices or tracks printed on one plastic body, kept apart by a gap, with link, button and plain rows and a selectable queue.
- Menu: First release. A menu button that opens Select's plastic panel of actions on the page body, flipping and shifting to stay whole, with a destructive action marked by a lamp and a word.
- Skeleton: First release. Skeleton holds the exact box of content still loading, drawn as the part with its power off (ghost segments, unlit holes, flat plastic). It never moves.
- TimePicker: First release. TimePicker sets a time of day in a recessed well. Hour and minute are spinbuttons with set keys under them, on the 24 or 12 hour clock.
